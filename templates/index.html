Sentiment Categories

Positive

Reviews expressing satisfaction, praise, enjoyment, or enthusiasm. High star ratings, glowing language, and strong recommendation signals.

"Brilliant film" "Loved every moment" "Must watch"

Negative

Reviews conveying disappointment, frustration, or criticism. Poor experiences, wasted time, and strong dissatisfaction.

"Terrible acting" "Waste of time" "Avoid this"

Neutral

Mixed opinions, balanced assessments, or factual descriptions without clear positive or negative bias.

"Decent enough" "Average film" "Some good parts"
