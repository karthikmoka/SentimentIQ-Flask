Dataset Information
Stanford Sentiment Treebank (SST-5)

Originally a five-class dataset (very negative → very positive) mapped to three classes:

  • Negative: SST labels 0 (very negative) + 1 (somewhat negative)
  • Neutral: SST label 2 (neutral)
  • Positive: SST labels 3 (somewhat positive) + 4 (very positive)
